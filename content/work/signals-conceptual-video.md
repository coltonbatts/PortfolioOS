## Project Overview

*Signals* is a personal experimental video exploring how we communicate in an age of constant transmission. It's less narrative, more visual essay—a three-minute meditation on connection, isolation, and the signals we send that never reach anyone.

## Concept & Inspiration

The piece is structured around the metaphor of radio transmission. We're all broadcasting constantly—social media posts, texts, emails, ambient data—but how much actually connects? How much is just noise?

Visually, I drew from:
- Early television test patterns
- Brutalist architecture (concrete and geometry as structure)
- Glitch art and data moshing
- Astronomical imagery (signals sent into space)

## Visual Approach

### Structure

The video is built in three acts:

1. **Transmission**: Clean signals, clear communication, optimistic
2. **Interference**: Glitches, noise, breakdown
3. **Static**: The void, silence, the space between transmissions

### Typography & Text

Minimal on-screen text, but when it appears, it's treated like data—scrolling code, fragmented phrases, words that decay before they finish forming. I used a monospace typeface to emphasize the digital/mechanical nature of language.

### Motion Language

Everything moves like signal waves—pulsing, expanding, fading. The camera never moves traditionally; instead, the frame itself shifts, distorts, and fractures. It's disorienting by design.

### Color

Monochrome base with occasional bursts of signal colors—red, blue, yellow—like test bars on old TVs. The piece lives mostly in blacks, whites, and grays, with color used sparingly for emphasis.

## Technical Execution

- **After Effects**: Core animation and compositing
- **Premiere Pro**: Editing and pacing
- **Photoshop**: Texture generation and static overlays
- **Glitch Tools**: Custom datamoshing and signal corruption
- **Audio**: Field recordings of radio static, morse code, dial tones

## Exhibition & Response

*Signals* has been shown in several digital art galleries and online video art platforms. The response has been surprisingly personal—people see their own anxieties about connection reflected in it.

## Why This Project Matters

I needed to make something without a client, without a brief, without constraints. *Signals* is pure creative exploration—a chance to experiment with visual language and see what happens when motion design meets conceptual art.

It's a reminder that not everything needs a practical purpose. Sometimes the work is just asking a question.
